A Letter For My Wifeee Jiii

Some feelings are too deep for ordinary words. This one is sealed with all my love.

💌
Our Little Universe • 8 November 2026

Meri Pyaari Muskan, Meri Pari...

Happy Birthday, Wifeee Jiii! ❤️

Today is the day the world was blessed with the gentlest, sweetest, most caring soul I have ever known. You are my best friend, my happiness, and my favourite person in this entire universe.

I watch you in awe every single day. You take care of me in ways I sometimes forget to take care of myself. Tum meri fikar mujhse zyada karti ho — maine kahan khaya, main soya ya maine aaram kiya, main pahucha ya nahi, main khush hoon ya nahi, kisi problem me toh nahi hoon... Tum apni favourite cheezein bhi mere according badal deti ho, meri pasand ko apni pasand bana leti ho, itne efforts karti ho sirf mere liye, mujhe khush rakhne ke liye! Aur mujhe sab dikhai deta hai, teri har choti si choti cheez notice kar leta hoon... kabhi kabhi bolta nahi hoon par iska yeh matlab nahi ki dikhai nahi deta. In a noisy world, your love is the only melody that brings stillness to my soul.

“I know I have made mistakes. I know there have been times when I hurt you or fell short. I never, ever want to take your forgiveness for granted. Main kabhi bhi tumhara mujhe maaf karne ko hawa me nahi liya. Tumhe hurt karke mujhe bhi bohot zyada dukh hota hai, main tumhe bohot pyaar karta hoon. I want to show my love through my everyday actions, not just words. Mera dil tumhe khush dekhna chahta hai, tumhe tumhari asli muskurahat se muskurata dekhna chahta hai, tumhe khush rakhna chahta hoon... Tumhe har dhalte din ke sath tumhara sath nibhana chahta hoon, har surya uday ke sath tera haath aur kas kar pakadna chahta hoon.”

To me, you are not just my partner — you are Meri Dharam Patni, the sacred anchor of my life. And you are Meri Personal Laxmi, because every blessing, peace, and warmth in my reality walked in the day you held my hand. Jab se tumne mera haath thama hai tab se meri zindagi badal gayi hai, meri zindagi ko tumne yun sanwara hai, mera har din tumne apna bana daala hai.

Thank you for being part of my world. Thank you for your cute childish laughter, your adorable little gussa, your pure white-rose heart, and for choosing to walk beside me since 02.04.2026.

Always & Forever Yours, Pyare Handsome sundar se pati dev, Yash 💙
P.S. You look breathtaking when you smile. Please never hide that smile from me. Now, come listen to what I recorded for you... 🎵
