A Letter For My Wifeee Jiii

Some feelings are too deep for ordinary words. This one is sealed with all my love.

💌
Our Little Universe • 8 November 2026

Meri Pyaari Muskan, Meri Pari...

Happy Birthday, Wifeee Jiii! ❤️

Today is the day the world was blessed with the gentlest, sweetest, most caring soul I have ever known. You are my best friend, my happiness, and my favourite person in this entire universe.

I watch you in awe every single day. You take care of me in ways I sometimes forget to take care of myself. You worry about whether I ate, whether I rested, and whether my heart is at peace. You change your little preferences just to bring a smile to my face, and your efforts never go unnoticed. In a noisy world, your love is the only melody that brings stillness to my soul.

“I know I have made mistakes. I know there have been times when I hurt you or fell short. I never, ever want to take your forgiveness for granted. I want to show my love through my everyday actions, not just words. My heart chooses to honour you, respect your boundaries, and earn your trust with every sunrise.”

To me, you are not just my partner — you are Meri Dharam Patni, the sacred anchor of my life. And you are Meri Personal Laxmi, because every blessing, peace, and warmth in my reality walked in the day you held my hand.

Thank you for being part of my world. Thank you for your cute childish laughter, your adorable little gussa, your pure white-rose heart, and for choosing to walk beside me since 02.04.2026.

Always & Forever Yours, Yash ❤️ Your Silly Husband-to-be
